How To Brew Pour-Over Coffee At Home: A Beginner's Guide

Pour over is an easy, foolproof method of brewing a great cup of coffee at home or in the office. Here we lay out our step-by-step guide for beginners on how to brew amazing pour-over coffee.

What you'll need:

Fresh Coffee Beans Burr grinder Pour Over Brewer (Hario V60, Kalita Wave) Paper Filter Gooseneck Kettle Scale Timer Mug/Carafe Hot Water (195°F - 205°F)

Step #1: Grind Your Coffee

Start by measuring out your coffee beans for a single cup. We like to go with anywhere from 15-18 grams of beans, depending on the type and roast level of bean we're using.

Once you've got enough grounds, measure it out carefully, then put in in a burr grinder on medium-coarse grind (about as fine as sea salt) while you heat your water to about 200°F. Freshly ground coffee is a must with pour over!

Step #2: Prepare Your Brewer

Now take the filter that came with your brewer and rinse it out with hot water, then dump any remaining water into a sink or coffee pot. You can use cold water for this step if you like, but we prefer hot since it removes paper taste.

Place the wet filter in the brewer and pour about an ounce of hot water over it to pre-heat everything. Give it another rinse and empty out all that water before adding your grounds!

Step #3: Add Coffee Grounds

Pour in your freshly ground coffee beans (with the burr grinder you just used), then gently shake the brewing vessel until the bed is even.

Step #4: Bloom The Coffee

Start a timer, then slowly pour about 1 ounce of hot water over the coffee grounds. Let it sit for 30-60 seconds to bloom and allow gases trapped in the beans to release before continuing with your brew.

Step #5: Pour More Water Slowly & Steadily

Slowly pour more hot water (about another 2 ounces) into a circular pattern around your bed of coffee grounds, avoiding pouring directly onto them. You want about 30-40 grams of total coffee in the brewer at this point.

Pour slowly and steadily until you've reached about 150-200 grams of water. Make sure everything is even as it pours, then let your brew begin!

Step #6: Let It Drip

Let all that delicious coffee drip through into the carafe or mug below for anywhere from 2:30 to 4 minutes total.

Step #7: Serve & Enjoy!

Once you've finished brewing, carefully remove the filter with grounds still on it. Give your cup a gentle swirl and enjoy your pour over coffee!

The more you brew, the more you'll learn which grind size, coffee-to-water ratio, pouring speed, and other variables to use for perfecting your cup of coffee.

Happy Brewing!
