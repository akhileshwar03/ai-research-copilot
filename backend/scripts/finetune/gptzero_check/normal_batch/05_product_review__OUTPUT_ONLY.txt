The XYZ Mechanical Keyboard is a solid performer and offers a great balance between features and price point in the mid-range of keyboards.

It's a joy to type on this one as it has tactile mechanical switches that give you a good amount of feedback but aren't too loud or grating while typing. The key travel feels just right, with enough resistance to prevent accidental presses when typing long pages of text but fluidity for those who spend time gaming or programming.

It's also well-built with a sturdy plastic chassis (albeit matte-finished) that resists fingerprints and light scratches. It doesn't have the premium heft of some of its aluminum-chassis counterparts, but it still feels solid enough to last every day.

The keycaps are PBT as they should be, which means you won't get any shine after a few months of use, plus the legends remain crisp for a long time too. It also comes with a USB-C cable that's able to snap on and off easily without breaking, meaning it'll stay intact even when you're tossing your backpack around.

Considering value-for-money, the XYZ Mechanical Keyboard is a steal at its price point. It has everything a mid-range mechanical keyboard should have such as customizable RGB backlighting, programmable keys, software support etc., though there are no hot-swappable switches or wireless connectivity options available here.

Overall, we're very impressed by this keyboard and would definitely recommend it to anyone looking for an affordable yet well-built mechanical keyboard.
