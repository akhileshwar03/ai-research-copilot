5 Small Habits That Quietly Improved My Sleep

Good sleep can feel like a luxury when life gets busy and hectic. Over time I found out making some subtle changes in how I lived improved the quality of my rest without having to make huge lifestyle overhauls.

These quiet habits quietly worked behind-the-scenes helping me fall asleep faster, get deeper into REM sleep and wake up feeling more refreshed than ever before!

Here are five small habits that made a big difference in my sleep:

1. Dimmed The Lights An Hour Before Bed

Bright lights, especially the kind coming from screens can trick your brain into thinking it's daytime. About an hour before bed I started dimming the lights around the house and turned off all electronic screens. This sends the signal to your brain that you need to wind down in order for your body to fall asleep easily.

2. Kept A Consistent Sleep Schedule

Even on weekends, sticking with a regular bedtime and wake up time helped regulate my internal clock so I could fall into natural sleep more quickly when it was time to go to bed at night instead of tossing and turning trying to catch some shuteye!

3. Drank Herbal Tea Instead Of Caffeine Late Afternoon

I made the switch from late afternoon coffee to caffeine free herbal tea in the evening. This small change helped reduce those stimulative effects that caffeine can have on your system so when it was time for bed you could more easily wind down.

4. Practiced A 5-Minute Gratitude Reflection Before Bed

Instead of falling asleep with the TV or computer still on I started a ritual of spending five minutes before lights out reflecting on three positive things from my day. It helps to put those worries and stresses behind me in order for me to feel calmer so that when it's time for sleep, I'm more able to relax.

5. Kept The Bedroom Cool And Comfortable

I've found a bedroom temperature between 60-67°F (15-19°C) is optimal for getting some good rest! Keeping the room cool helped me fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer than ever before!
