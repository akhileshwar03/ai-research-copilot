Starting Your Day Off Right: Morning Routines Help Set The Tone

Posted by Ryan Squires on Sep 14th, 2022

As with any aspect of productivity or performance improvement techniques like those that we've shared here in the past (see our productivity guide, for instance), establishing a strong start to each day can help your entire week go much more smoothly. That's not just because taking time at the beginning sets you up for success later on, but also because it provides motivation and momentum throughout an otherwise chaotic workday.

In fact, some would say that there are few more significant things than starting off right in life—particularly as a person who is looking to establish better habits of every sort. What you do with your time each morning can have major ramifications for how the rest of that day plays out. In turn, if someone establishes such a healthy and productive routine early on, it helps set them up much more effectively throughout their career.

Morning Routines: Why Should They Exist?

Some would suggest starting off right isn't just an old-fashioned piece of advice—that making sure you begin your waking hours in positive, energizing ways can really help establish habits that serve the mind and body well. In turn, many people choose to make time for a healthy start through exercise, meditation or prayer, and journaling.

Of course some may suggest those are more extreme behaviors—like going to sleep early at night, meditating twice daily, starting an entire mindfulness practice, doing push-ups while your eyes open, etc—but all can help establish the right mind-set. At any rate, many people who begin with such activities find it helps them become energized quickly and get back on track for tasks or goals they want to accomplish each day.

For instance, many of us have had an especially draining night's sleep due to an unusually bad cold—how much harder is your morning than usual? That feeling can permeate the rest of a day. Conversely, if you start with healthy habits and begin waking refreshed (whether from a great night or by taking time for good stretching in bed), it goes far toward giving our minds more momentum throughout each week.

Ultimately then, establishing positive morning routines isn't just an empty cliché—it's backed up by research that shows how having productive starting habits helps us become better at work and life. Not only can a routine like that help us begin on the right foot but doing so helps reduce decision fatigue (the tendency to get worn down because our minds are overburdened with too many choices), which is why some people have found such programs useful for boosting focus, energy levels in general, or mood states.

Of course there's always going to be a certain degree of individuality involved in figuring out how your routine should work—just look at all the different morning ritual apps and productivity frameworks you might choose. But no matter what your daily plan is, if it helps establish that productive starting habit then go for whatever approach you can find or build!
