The Apex Pro Mid-Range Mechanical Keyboard surprises with how polished it feels for its price. It hits a sweet spot between high-end performance and everyday affordability. As soon as you start typing, the brown-tactile switches stand out. They give a soft bump with each press, so you get clear feedback without the loud clicking sound some keyboards have. This makes it comfortable for long typing sessions, but it still responds quickly enough for gaming. The keycaps are made from double-shot PBT, which means they don’t get shiny or lose their texture even after heavy use. Plus, their slightly curved shape guides your fingers naturally across the keys. The build is solid, too. The sturdy aluminum frame has a brushed finish that cuts down on vibrations, and the USB-C cable detaches, which is handy. The stabilizers on the bigger keys come well-lubed right out of the box, so there’s hardly any rattling—something you don’t always find at this price. On top of that, it has extra features like programmable macro keys, per-key RGB lighting, and a simple software suite that lets you customize settings without a steep learning curve. It’s priced between $100 and $120, giving you perks usually reserved for more expensive models but without the premium cost. All in all, the Apex Pro shows that a mid-range mechanical keyboard can deliver satisfying typing, sturdy build quality, and useful features that make it worth the price for both pros and hobbyists.
