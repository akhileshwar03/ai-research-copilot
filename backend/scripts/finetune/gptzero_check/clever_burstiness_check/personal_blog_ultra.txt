Quitting your day job is always terrifying — you never quite know where things are going to land after making such an impulsive decision. For months though, the idea of quitting hasn't left me alone: as someone with freelance experience (not many people can say they have freelanced while in college), I have been pondering whether or not it would be feasible for my current 9-to-5 job and decided to go into a little more depth on what actually happens when you decide that being self-employed is the way forward.

It wasn't an easy choice. The safety of stability is something hard to pass up, after all: your money, in return for you putting off sleep (or just plain working) day-to-day, will stay the same from week to month as long as your employers can pay their staff wages — and that's not even mentioning perks like health insurance or life assurance.

But below this cushion of routine is also a whole different world than most people choose. It feels like it is there calling out just slightly too often: I want more creative freedom, personal independence in how to run the company (or freelancing) itself…

A lot of questions and little answers

The idea sounded alluring: the ability to wake up every morning excited about your projects that you get to choose; coffee shops and a comfortable home office were part of my mental picture along with a perfect work-life balance. However, doubts crept in on me quickly: would I be able to find enough clients? Could I deal with the erratic income? What if I failed?

So many nights thinking about it and discussing with people who did likewise. At some point I realized my fear of regret was stronger than that of failure (which is strange to me). You never know what life will dish out you so the safest bet isn't necessarily your most satisfying one...I've been building up an emergency fund, putting money into a little portfolio and networking amongst folks in freelancing communities.

Today marked a big change for me - I handed in my resignation.

It's bizarre how strange the emotion can be when it finally hits you. I'm feeling so relieved yet energized by the same experience that sends chills down my spine. It feels like I'm stepping off into an uncharted world, but am fully geared up with passion and preparation... ready to chase a career of my own making.

I've had this dream job since high school - I just never thought it would be possible to take full advantage of it until now. Now the gears are set in motion so let's see how far we can go from here!

Here comes the exciting life-changing journey that everyone wants but few attempt! We're chasing after dreams and being freelance together one gig at a time.
