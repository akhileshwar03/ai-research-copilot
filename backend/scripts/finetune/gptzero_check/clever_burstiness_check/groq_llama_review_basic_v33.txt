The new Apex Pro Mid-Range Mechanical Keyboard surprises you with how polished it feels for the price. It hits a sweet spot between high-end performance and affordability that works for daily use. The brown-tactile switches give you a soft bump with every keypress, so you know it’s registering without the loud noise you get from some other switches. That makes typing for hours easier while still giving gamers the quick response they want.

The keycaps are made from double-shot PBT plastic, which means they won’t get shiny or lose their texture even after heavy use. Their slightly curved shape helps your fingers find the right keys naturally. The keyboard itself feels sturdy, built with a strong aluminum frame that has a brushed finish to reduce vibrations. You also get a detachable USB-C cable, which is a handy addition. The stabilizers on the bigger keys come pre-lubed, so there’s hardly any rattle—something many keyboards in this range overlook.

You’ll find extras like programmable macro keys, per-key RGB lighting, and straightforward software that makes setting up profiles easy without a steep learning curve. Priced between $100 and $120, this keyboard packs in features you usually see in more expensive models but without the premium price. The Apex Pro proves a mid-range mechanical keyboard can deliver a great typing feel, solid build, and useful features, making it a smart pick whether you’re a pro or just into keyboards.
