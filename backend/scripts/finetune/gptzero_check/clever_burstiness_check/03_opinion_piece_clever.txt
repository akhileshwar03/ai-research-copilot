Open plan offices have been popular in the past couple of years, they are thought to make employees communicate more openly, and shatter the hierarchy in the workplace.

While the intention of an open plan office is to bring employees together to collaborate, they can also cause a lack of productivity.

An open plan office means there are no barriers between employees, while that can make it easier to talk to a coworker it can also mean more distractions in the workplace. Employees need to be able to get into a flow of productive work, which an open plan office can make extremely difficult. Constant buzzing of phones, and conversations can distract employees from their task at hand. This can have the opposite effect on the employees of what the company intended.

Some studies have shown that employees are happier working in closed off or private offices.

Although some employers might not agree, an open-plan office can hurt employee productivity. In a study done in 2018, employees stated that noise and a lack of privacy in open offices decreased their productivity, and caused them to be more stressed. Open office layouts can interrupt the flow of productive work, causing employees to become distracted.

While the idea of an open office is to bring people together, it can make it harder to collaborate effectively. Most of the time it is better for an employee to focus on a task at hand, then to have someone come up to them and ask for help or to collaborate.

Collaboration in the workplace should be planned, not spontaneous. Employees need to have space to focus on a task, without any outside distractions, then to come together to collaborate this is not an easy process to set up.

An office with a variety of work spaces can be optimal in the workplace, for different types of tasks, employees can choose where they feel most comfortable working.

An open plan office can do the opposite of what is intended, instead of boosting productivity, it hurts it. Distracting noises and a lack of privacy can lower an employees focus. Making it more difficult to collaborate can also reduce productivity. Employees should be able to work in the way they feel is best for them, an office with a variety of work spaces can boost productivity while still encouraging teamwork
