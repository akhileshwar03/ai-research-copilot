The Apex Pro Mid-Range Mechanical Keyboard offers a refined experience that stands out at its price. Its brown-tactile switches deliver a subtle bump with each press, avoiding the loud click of other types while still providing enough feedback for gamers who need quick responses. This makes it comfortable for long typing sessions without sacrificing precision. The keycaps are made from double-shot PBT, which prevents shine and keeps their texture even after heavy use. Their slightly concave shape helps fingers move naturally across the keys. The build feels solid, thanks to a sturdy aluminum frame with a brushed finish that reduces vibrations. Plus, the detachable USB-C cable adds convenience. The larger keys come with factory-lubed stabilizers that minimize rattling—a detail often missed at this level. For its cost, the keyboard packs in extras such as programmable macro keys, per-key RGB lighting, and straightforward software that allows easy profile customization. Priced between $100 and $120, it offers features typically reserved for more expensive models but without the high price. In all, the Apex Pro delivers a satisfying typing experience, durable build, and useful features that make it a strong option for both work and play.
