The Apex Pro Mid-Range Mechanical Keyboard offers a refined experience that stands out given its price. Its brown-tactile switches provide a subtle bump with each keystroke, delivering enough feedback for gaming without the loud noise common to other switches. This makes typing for extended periods comfortable while still maintaining responsiveness. The keycaps, made from double-shot PBT plastic, resist wear and maintain their texture even after extensive use. Their slightly concave shape helps fingers move smoothly across the keys. The keyboard’s build is solid, featuring an aluminum frame with a brushed finish that reduces vibrations. It also includes a detachable USB-C cable, which adds practical convenience. The larger keys come equipped with factory-lubed stabilizers that minimize rattle, a detail often missing in this price range. Additional features include programmable macro keys, per-key RGB lighting, and a straightforward software suite that allows easy customization without complexity. Priced between $100 and $120, it offers capabilities typically found in more expensive models while avoiding a premium cost. The Apex Pro manages to combine dependable construction, enjoyable typing, and useful features, making it a strong option both for professionals and enthusiasts.
