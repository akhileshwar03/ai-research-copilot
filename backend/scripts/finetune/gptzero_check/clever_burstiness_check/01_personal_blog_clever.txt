I have been debating whether to quit my job for the past few months. The thought of completely quitting my job and becoming a freelancer scares and excites me at the same time. Although a regular job provides financial stability, I have felt lately that I need to break free from the daily routine. I have always dreamt of being able to work from anywhere and have total control over the projects I do. However, I did not know if this was a feasible option for me to consider

It took me a while to come to terms with making such a big decision. I spoke to a few friends that had taken up freelancing and inquired about my options. After a while, I came to the conclusion that it was better to fail at freelancing than to regret working nine to five forever

So, I started preparing for this moment by setting money aside and finding contacts in the industry

Today, as I hand in my resignation, I am filled with mixed emotions. I am glad to be taking the first step in my journey toward becoming a freelancer. Working remotely gives me so many opportunities, and I hope to make the best out of it.
