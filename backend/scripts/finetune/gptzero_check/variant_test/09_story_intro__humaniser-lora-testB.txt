The kitchen smelled like cinnamon and old wood as it wrapped itself around me while perched on a stool beside Grandma's worn oak table... The morning light filtered through the lace curtains casting delicate patterns over her flour-dusted countertops. Grandma's hands moved with effortless grace cracking eggs into folding batter as if performing their own well-rehearsed dance. I watched wide-eyed trying to mimic every movement, though my fingers fumbled and sometimes eggshells slipped in... "Cooking is more than just following a recipe," she said softly smiling while catching the clumsy attempt of me to whisk everything together, "...It's also how you tell stories, sharing love without words..."

Her voice was so warm and steady like an anchor in whirlwind of sizzling pans & bubbling pots. I can still remember her humming old songs as she stirred or sparkle when our kitchen filled with aroma from caramelizing onions, fresh baked bread... That summer it seems the kitchen became OUR haven, where time slowed just for us and lessons could stretch beyond measuring cups to 350*.

Each dish had a memory; a piece of her past woven into this present. From burnt cookies to perfect pies I began learning that when you learn how-to cook from Grandma... It wasn't about food but rather heritage and patience, the quiet joy she got while creating something with her own hands. And then as sun dipped below horizon casting golden glow over cluttered countertops... I realized was inheriting more than recipes..legacy.
