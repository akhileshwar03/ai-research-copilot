Hasn't there been at least one point in your life when you stood on a high building ledge or by some windowsill where it was possible to gaze upon that most wondrous sight, the sky turning into a shade of red so warm and beautiful?

It's something all people look forward too. What is causing such an astounding display? The answer lies within.

During the day time when the sun is situated high in the sky what you are seeing goes through little scattering as it travels for only shorter distance while passing through our atmosphere. Sunlight actually comprises of many colours ( red, orange , yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet ) which we mix together to form white light. The atmosphere scatters some color when this light enters them mainly the short wavelength such a as blueness so that's why you see sky mostly being blue during day time.

But now it's sunset! At what point the sun is lower in horizon its light has travelled through more thickness of our Atmosphere before hitting your eyes. So at night, most part of Blue color and violet one are scattered out from line of sight as these wavelengths aren't able to travel long distance so that only those colours left which have longer wavelength like reds oranges yewllows and they continue straight without being scattered easily.

That's how the sky near horizon glows with warm red and orange. Sometimes even more because all particle in the air dust pollution, water drop etc also helps them scatter these shorter wavelengths much better!

So every time next you see fiery red sunset remember it is all because of the way sunlight dances through our atmosphere and scatters colours painting a beautiful canvas just above your head everyday at night. How amazing isn't that?
