As twilight melts slowly into nightfall, the beating heart of old town Chiang Mai awakens from its slumber to unleash a torrential outpouring of energy that sweeps locals on their feet as much as visitors; and that's if they manage to find their way off the beaten track in time for the nightly advent.

The cobbled pedestrian street comes alive with vibrant colour as strings of lanterns spill golden light over stalls brimming with all manner of exotic delights: silky fabrics, fragrant spices, glittering treasures of silver crafted by hand, or perhaps a rustic wooden carving from across China. The air is thick with the intoxicating aroma of sizzling hot food and freshly sliced fruits; lemongrass mingling with the sharp whisperings of chilli powder, sticky rice turning to caramelisation under coconut cream as grilled pineapple sits seductively over bamboo skewers on charcoal fire.

There's a gentle hum that builds around you – bartering voices rise in crescendo with bursts of laughter and the occasional sweet tinkle of guitar strings; your senses are tickled by every movement made. Hands beckon, cheeks flushed, grills sizzle and flip with enthusiasm, as vendors pull their faces into beaming smiles to entice customers into the glow of a well-kept fire.

Lush fabrics drape in all manner of jewel tones, shimmering like silk on the uneven cobbles beneath your feet. The soft rustle is complemented by bursts of spicy heat and sweet caramelisation as sizzling dishes hit hot grills – and as they reach their peak they're expertly flipped onto a large banana leaf to be served just as you arrive for a bite of sticky rice or grilled pork loin that's been seasoned to perfection with the finest ingredients. A young man slices his fruits in front of your very eyes, holding the perfectly shaped wedge out for a final dip – it's simply delicious.

You run a finger along a length of intricate embroidery woven from scratch by skilled hands over many months; feel the softness as a silk cloth drapes across your wrist and let yourself be entranced by its vibrant colours. As you turn to continue, the uneven cobbles beneath your feet rumble with chatter: sandals shuffle across stone, shoes click on pavement – all around you are people weaving in from every angle.

Overhead straws hiss as coconut milk spits into a large copper vessel; nearby a man deftly flips mangos over charcoal fire before heaving the sticky concoction to your table. The fragrance permeates Chiang Mai's night air while the sounds of life bubble and weave all around you – in one corner, people gather to catch up on the day's gossip or simply watch as they sip jasmine tea from delicately formed porcelain mugs.

A child runs past in between stalls before turning back for a playful shout that rises above the chatter: a young family sits together with ease at an open air table sipping local coffee while children play; old men sit calmly and wait until night's end to retire home – they simply have no intention of hurrying as jasmine scents their tea.

A stroll through Chiang Mai's night market is both immersive dance of senses, vibrant mélange of sight, sound and scent that tickles every facet of your existence: an exquisite mosaic made with love where you're pulled irresistibly closer to the living soul at heart of Northern Thailand's nighttime life.
