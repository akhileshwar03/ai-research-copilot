XYZ Mechanical Keyboard

The Mechanical Keyboards are here to save us from our mouse clicking woes! There's just no arguing with the benefits of going full-on typing as opposed to mousing your way through work and life.

You might not be familiar with XYZ yet, but let me assure you they're the genuine article. They make a number or mechanical keyboards that we've used in this guide before and I was pleasantly surprised by their first product from this range - the XYZ Mechanical Keyboard.

Mechanical Switches

One of my pet peeves is a keyboard that's too quiet to type on as if I'm an octopus who just wants to crawl into bed after work, it has to be nice and loud. This keyboard isn't overly loud in any way but there certainly is enough sound when you tap them - the good thing about this board being mechanical of course!

If you haven't heard a mechanical switch before then all we can say without spoiling too much away is that they respond wonderfully well when pressed. There's not even anything like noise to be dealt with as you would get if you went for some rubber dome options, in fact I have found the travel on these switches makes them perfect for long typing sessions - it means I'm still going strong after an hour!

Build Quality

The build quality is where this keyboard really comes into its own. The plastic chassis has a matte finish and manages to hold up extremely well against fingerprints as you're sure they'll be all over the board before too much time passes.

Even if you don't have any fancy extras or special key caps, it feels like the kind of thing that will last many years without showing signs of wear in terms of durability anyway. My only niggle with how solid this keyboard is would probably just be for someone who wanted to pick up an aluminium board instead - otherwise I'd call it very good quality indeed.

RGB

As mentioned above, the main features on offer when you're picking these things up will come down to what you want from them and we've included everything from a few basic hot keys that most boards can do anyway all of the way through to high-end custom RGB with many different lighting effects. In between those two extremes there's something for everyone I would say, XYZ Mechanical Keyboards tick off pretty much every box in their range - if you want it chances are they have it!

The XYZ board is one that has RGB backlighting and as good a way of demonstrating the quality is by running through some simple on-screen tests using the pre-installed software included. The LEDs aren't particularly bright but for your average user, I don't think anything will come across better in terms of value-for-money. It would have been nice to get hot-swappable switches or wireless capability too though!

Price

Speaking about cost and value-for-money - I just wanted it on the record that this board ticks all those right boxes as well! In fact, there aren't many areas where you can find something with so much in its price range. From the keycaps up to some of its RGB backlighting features they're basically included everything people will need or want from their mechanical keyboard - and yet how good would it be if a company came along and sold this board at half-price? It just wouldn't feel quite right now, oh wait...
