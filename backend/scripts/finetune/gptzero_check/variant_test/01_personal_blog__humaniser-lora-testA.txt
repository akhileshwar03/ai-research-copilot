For months now have been wrestling over whether or not it's worth quitting my stable 9-5 job for freelancing full-time.

It wasn't an easy decision I made lightly; stability is such a comfort – the steady income that comes with having your own job, the predictability of waking up knowing exactly what you need to get done and all those benefits. But underneath it was gnawing at me…a yearning for creative freedom in exchange.

The idea had always intrigued me: waking each day excited about work I choose; working from my favorite coffee shop or snuggled into a cozy home office, the ability of balancing life with freelancing on your terms…

But doubts loomed large – would you find enough clients? Could manage unpredictability income. What if it all falls apart?

I spent countless late nights thinking and talking to friends who had made similar leaps in faith.

In my heart I knew that fear regret is worse than the fear failure…life so unpredictable, sometimes safest path isn't most fulfilling…

So with a plan set for saving an emergency fund; building portfolio and networking freelancing communities – here we go!

I handed over letter today. It feels surreal: relief excitement nerves all at once.

Stepping into unknown armed passion preparation

This new chapter is about growth learning carving out career truly mine…
